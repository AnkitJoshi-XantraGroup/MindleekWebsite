# 🚀 Quick Start - Deploy to Hostinger in 5 Minutes

## ⚡ Fast Track Deployment

### Step 1: Download Your Files (1 minute)
Download these 4 essential files from your repository:
- ✅ `index.html`
- ✅ `styles.css`
- ✅ `script.js`
- ✅ `favicon.png`

### Step 2: Log into Hostinger (1 minute)
1. Go to [hostinger.com](https://www.hostinger.com)
2. Click "Login"
3. Enter your credentials

### Step 3: Upload Files (2 minutes)
1. Click **"File Manager"** in your hosting dashboard
2. Open the **`public_html`** folder
3. Delete any existing default files
4. Click **"Upload"**
5. Select and upload all 4 files
6. Wait for upload to complete ✓

### Step 4: Test Your Site (1 minute)
1. Open your domain in a browser
2. Your website is now LIVE! 🎉

## 🔧 Need to Make Changes?

### Update Text:
1. Edit `index.html` with any text editor
2. Re-upload to Hostinger
3. Refresh your browser

### Change Colors:
1. Edit `styles.css` (change values in `:root` section)
2. Re-upload to Hostinger
3. Refresh your browser
